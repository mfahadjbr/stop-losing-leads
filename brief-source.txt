
SLLHQ Website Rebuild Brief
Oct 6, 2026 · @Someone
The refresh at sll-leads.vercel.app is a new coat of paint on the same page. The headline, problem section, stats, quiz, three tiers, three testimonials and FAQ are word for word the live site, so it will convert about the same. The site does not fail because of how it looks. It fails because a cold visitor meets a faceless company, sees prices before anyone has asked about their business, and gets six different things to click.
The fix is a StoryBrand rebuild: the visitor is the hero, our team is the guide (co founders Kevin, Marcie and Jordanna, with our AI agents Mav and River and the team behind them), there is ONE ask (a Lead Leak Diagnostic conversation), and the tiers move behind that conversation where they become the answer to what the business actually needs.
Diagnosis
Seven problems sit on both versions. The refresh fixed none of them. The problem section (phone rings, nobody picks up) is the strongest copy on the site and should stay.

Patterns to copy
1. The hero names a specific buyer and outcome ("for the trades," "turn your Thumbtack leads into booked jobs"), never the tech.
2. One main ask repeated four or five times, plus one low pressure second door (Ylopo: book a demo, or estimate your ROI).
3. A self diagnosis tool as the lead magnet. Our quiz already does this, so keep it and make it end in the diagnostic.
4. Proof with names, dollars and time frames. The two small agencies with no proof are the ones to beat, and one real named client already beats them.
5. A before and after or "us vs. them" table.
Gaps we can own
• Real founders with a real story. Nobody tells one. Kevin, Marcie, Jordanna, Mav, River and the team are the advantage no software company can copy.
• Done for you AND you own it. Software sells tools, and agencies sell installs with no proof. Our position: "We build it, run it, and you own it."
• Database reactivation for service businesses. Only Happy Grasshopper markets it, and only to real estate. A "what's sitting in your old contacts" angle is open.
• A human diagnostic, not a bot scan. Every rival audit is automated. A conversation with the founders is the premium version.
On pricing: Price shows only on offers under about $300 a month. Every done for you or high touch seller in the set hides it behind a call. At $675 to $1,160 we are in the hide it group, which matches your instinct.

Discovery, not a pitch: where the tiers go
The tiers come off the homepage and become the prescription we write after the diagnostic. The visitor learns which leak they have; the tier name shows up only once we know it fits.
Where
What it shows
Homepage
No tier cards, no a la carte menu, no dollar amounts. The FAQ says pricing is covered on the diagnostic.
Quiz results
Names the leak, not a product. Three results: "Speed leak" (slow or missed replies), "Follow up leak" (one touch, then silence), "Database leak" (old contacts never worked). Each ends with "Book your diagnostic and we'll show you how to close it."
Diagnostic call
We map their lead flow live, then match the leak to the fix. Speed leak maps to Response Rescue, follow up leak to Revenue Accelerator, all three plus reporting to Revenue Protection Pro.
After the call
We send a private proposal for the plan we recommend, with their own numbers from the call and the price. The old public tier pages come down (or go unlisted and hidden from Google) so prices are not findable on the site.
Business Lab
Shown on the site as the community every client joins, and as the room for owners not ready for done for you. That gives visitors who won't book a call somewhere to go.
This matches the market. Every done for you seller at our price hides the price behind a call, and our diagnostic becomes the real product people want: a free look at their business from the team that builds the system.
