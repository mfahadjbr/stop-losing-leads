• Headline: You already paid for the lead. Let's make sure somebody answers it.
• Subhead: Most service businesses lose good leads to a missed call or a slow reply. We build and run the follow up system that answers every lead in seconds, so you book more jobs from the leads you already have.
• Button: Book My Lead Leak Diagnostic
• Second door, as a text link: Not ready to talk? Take the 30 second Lead Leak Quiz.
• Visual: a real photo of the team at work (Kevin, Marcie, Jordanna), not stock and not an illustration. Caption: "The team behind Stop Losing Leads HQ."
2. The problem (villain plus external and internal problem)
Keep the four cards from the current site (phone rings and nobody picks up, form sits overnight, one reply then silence, old contacts collecting dust). They are the best copy we have. Change the headline and the closing line:

• Closing line: You are on the job, doing the work you're great at. That is exactly when the phone rings. It's not a people problem. It's a system problem.

Our own team runs on two AI agents we built, Mav for strategy and River for getting the work done, so every system we install is one we use every day.
